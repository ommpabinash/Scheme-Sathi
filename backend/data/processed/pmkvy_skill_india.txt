SCHEME TITLE: Pmkvy Skill India
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Free industry-relevant skill training, certification, and job placement.
Eligibility Criteria:
Unemployed youth or school/college dropouts (aged 15-45).
Exclusion Criteria:
Currently employed.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Educational Marksheet.
