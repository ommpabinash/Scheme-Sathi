SCHEME TITLE: Credit Guarantee Fund
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/agmsmercgt

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Scheme for Micro/Small Enterprises)
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Collateral-free credit facility up to Rs. 5 Crore for MSMEs.
Eligibility Criteria:
New and existing Micro and Small Enterprises.
Exclusion Criteria:
Retail trade beyond limits.
Documents Required:
MSME Udyam Registration, Loan Application.

SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof
