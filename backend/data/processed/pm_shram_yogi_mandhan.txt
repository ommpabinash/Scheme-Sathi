SCHEME TITLE: PM Shram Yogi Mandhan
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 for unorganized workers (monthly income <= 15,000).
Eligibility Criteria:
Unorganized workers aged 18-40 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
EPFO/NPS/ESIC members.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Savings Bank Account.
