SCHEME TITLE: PM Vishwakarma Skill
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Vishwakarma Basic & Advanced Skill Training
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
5-7 days basic skill training with Rs. 500/day stipend for traditional craftsmen.
Eligibility Criteria:
Artisans registered under PM Vishwakarma.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-registered.
Documents Required:
PM Vishwakarma ID, Aadhaar.
