SCHEME TITLE: Ladli Behna MP
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana (Madhya Pradesh)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Monthly financial assistance of Rs. 1,250 transferred to women's bank accounts.
Eligibility Criteria:
Women domiciled in MP aged 21-60 years with family income < 2.5 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Taxpaying families.
Documents Required:
Samagra ID, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
