Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Targeted 20-40% reduction in particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10) concentrations in 131 cities.

Residents of non-attainment Indian cities.
