SCHEME TITLE: Pmay Gramin
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmuy2

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Assistance of Rs. 1.2-1.3 Lakh for rural homeless households to build pucca houses.
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural households identified in SECC 2011 list.
Exclusion Criteria:
Kutcha house non-listed.
Documents Required:
Job Card, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
