SCHEME TITLE: Udyam Registration
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/sprs

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Udyam MSME Registration Portal
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Free official registration providing government tender priority & interest subsidies.
Eligibility Criteria:
All Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises.
Exclusion Criteria:
Unregistered trading entities.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, PAN Card, GSTIN.
