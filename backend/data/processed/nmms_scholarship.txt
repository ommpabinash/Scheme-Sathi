SCHEME TITLE: Nmms Scholarship
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMSS)
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Rs. 12,000/year scholarship for class 9-12 students to prevent dropouts.
Eligibility Criteria:
Class 8 students (55%+ marks) with family income <= 3.5 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
KVS/NVS school students.
Documents Required:
Class 8 Marksheet, Income Certificate.

SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
