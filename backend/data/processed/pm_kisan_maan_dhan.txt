SCHEME TITLE: PM Kisan Maan Dhan
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Kisan Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-KMY)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Assured monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 for small and marginal farmers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Small and marginal farmers aged 18-40 years with <= 2 hectares land.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income tax payers, NPS/EPFO members.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account.
