SCHEME TITLE: Poshan Vaticana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Poshan 2.0 Anganwadi Services
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Provides supplementary nutrition, pre-school non-formal education to children & mothers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Children (6 months - 6 yrs), pregnant & lactating women.
Exclusion Criteria:
None.
Documents Required:
Anganwadi Registration, Aadhaar.
