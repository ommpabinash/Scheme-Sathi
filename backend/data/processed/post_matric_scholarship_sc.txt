SCHEME TITLE: Post Matric Scholarship Sc
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmsfss

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST Students
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Tuition waiver and maintenance allowance for SC/ST post-secondary education.
Eligibility Criteria:
SC/ST students with family income <= Rs. 2.5 Lakh/year.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income > 2.5 Lakh.
Documents Required:
Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, Marksheet.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
