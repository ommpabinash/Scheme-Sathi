SCHEME TITLE: Rashtriya Vayoshri
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Free physical aids and assisted-living devices for low-income senior citizens.
Eligibility Criteria:
Senior citizens aged 60+ belonging to BPL category.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-BPL senior citizens.
Documents Required:
BPL Card, Age Proof, Aadhaar.
