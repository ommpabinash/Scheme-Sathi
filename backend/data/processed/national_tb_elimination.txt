SCHEME TITLE: National TB Elimination
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Nikshay Poshan Yojana (TB Patients)
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Financial support of Rs. 500/month for nutritional needs of TB patients.
Eligibility Criteria:
All notified TB patients undergoing treatment.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-notified private unrecorded cases.
Documents Required:
Nikshay ID, Aadhaar, Bank Account.
