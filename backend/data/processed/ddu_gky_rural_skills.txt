SCHEME TITLE: Ddu Gky Rural Skills
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Placement-linked skill training for rural poor youth (aged 15-35).
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural youth from poor families (SC/ST/Minority priority).
Exclusion Criteria:
Urban youth.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, BPL Proof, Education Certificate.
