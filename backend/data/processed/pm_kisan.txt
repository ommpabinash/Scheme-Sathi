SCHEME TITLE: PM Kisan
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-kisan

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Kisan Samman Nidhi
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Income support of Rs. 6,000/year to landholding farmers in 3 installments.
Eligibility Criteria:
All landholding farmer families.
Exclusion Criteria:
Institutional landholders, taxpayers, MPs/MLAs.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Land Records, Bank Account.
