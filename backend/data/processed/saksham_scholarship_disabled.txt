SCHEME TITLE: Saksham Scholarship Disabled
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
AICTE Saksham Scholarship for Disabled Students
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Rs. 50,000/year for specially-abled students pursuing technical education.
Eligibility Criteria:
Differently-abled students (disability >= 40%) with income <= 8 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Disability < 40%.
Documents Required:
Disability Certificate, Admission Proof.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:

• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
