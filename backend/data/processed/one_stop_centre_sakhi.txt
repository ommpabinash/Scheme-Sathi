SCHEME TITLE: One Stop Centre Sakhi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
One Stop Centre (Sakhi)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Provides integrated support and assistance to women affected by violence.
Eligibility Criteria:
Women facing physical, sexual, emotional, or economic abuse.
Exclusion Criteria:
None.
Documents Required:
Identity Proof, Complaint details.
