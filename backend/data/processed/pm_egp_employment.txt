SCHEME TITLE: PM Egp Employment
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Credit-linked subsidy up to 35% for setting up new micro-enterprises.
Eligibility Criteria:
Individuals above 18 years; minimum 8th pass for manufacturing > 10L.
Exclusion Criteria:
Existing units.
Documents Required:
Project Report, Aadhaar, Caste Certificate.
