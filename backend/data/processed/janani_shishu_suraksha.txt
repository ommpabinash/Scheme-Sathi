SCHEME TITLE: Janani Shishu Suraksha
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/jssk

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Free medical treatment, transport, and diet for sick newborns & mothers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Pregnant women and sick infants accessing public health facilities.
Exclusion Criteria:
Private hospitals.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Hospital Registration.
