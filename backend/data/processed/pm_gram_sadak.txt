SCHEME TITLE: PM Gram Sadak
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Provides all-weather road connectivity to eligible unconnected rural habitations.
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural habitations with population 500+ (250+ in hills).
Exclusion Criteria:
Already connected habitations.
Documents Required:
N/A (Infrastructure).
