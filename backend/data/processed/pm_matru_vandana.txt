SCHEME TITLE: PM Matru Vandana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Cash incentive of Rs. 5,000 for 1st child & Rs. 6,000 for 2nd girl child to pregnant mothers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Pregnant & Lactating Mothers meeting social criteria.
Exclusion Criteria:
Regular government employees.
Documents Required:
MCP Card, Aadhaar, Bank Passbook.
