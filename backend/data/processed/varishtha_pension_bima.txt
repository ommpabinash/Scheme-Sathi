SCHEME TITLE: Varishtha Pension Bima
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Pension scheme for senior citizens offering guaranteed 7.4%+ annual return.
Eligibility Criteria:
Senior citizens aged 60 years and above.
Exclusion Criteria:
Age < 60.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, PAN Card, Deposit Amount.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
