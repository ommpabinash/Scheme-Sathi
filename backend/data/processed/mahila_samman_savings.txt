SCHEME TITLE: Mahila Samman Savings
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mssc

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Small savings scheme for women offering 7.5% fixed interest for 2 years.
Eligibility Criteria:
Any woman or guardian on behalf of a minor girl.
Exclusion Criteria:
Male applicants.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, PAN Card, Deposit Amount.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:

• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof
