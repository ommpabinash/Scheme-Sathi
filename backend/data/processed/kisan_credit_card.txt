SCHEME TITLE: Kisan Credit Card
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/kcc

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Concessional credit to farmers for agricultural & allied activity expenses.
Eligibility Criteria:
Farmers, individual/joint borrowers, SHGs, tenant farmers.
Exclusion Criteria:
Defaulters to bank credit.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, PAN Card, Land ownership proof.
