SCHEME TITLE: Kanyashree Prakalpa WB
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Kanyashree Prakalpa (West Bengal)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Annual scholarship of Rs. 1,000 and one-time grant of Rs. 25,000 to prevent child marriage.
Eligibility Criteria:
Unmarried girls aged 13-19 studying in recognized institutions in WB.
Exclusion Criteria:
Married girls under 18.
Documents Required:
School Certificate, Unmarried Certificate, Aadhaar.
