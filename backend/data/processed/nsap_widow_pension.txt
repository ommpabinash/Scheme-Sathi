SCHEME TITLE: Nsap Widow Pension
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension (IGNWPS)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Monthly pension of Rs. 300-500 for destitute widows.
Eligibility Criteria:
BPL widows aged 40-79 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Remarried widows / Non-BPL.
Documents Required:
BPL Card, Death Certificate of Husband, Aadhaar.
