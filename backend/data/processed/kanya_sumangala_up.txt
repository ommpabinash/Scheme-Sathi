SCHEME TITLE: Kanya Sumangala UP
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Mukhya Mantri Kanya Sumangala Yojana (Uttar Pradesh)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Rs. 25,000 phased financial assistance for girl child from birth to graduation.
Eligibility Criteria:
Girls domiciled in UP with annual family income <= 3 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Families with > 2 children.
Documents Required:
Birth Certificate, Income Proof, Domicile.
