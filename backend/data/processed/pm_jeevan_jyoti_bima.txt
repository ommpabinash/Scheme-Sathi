SCHEME TITLE: PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Life insurance cover of Rs. 2 Lakh for any cause of death at Rs. 436/year.
Eligibility Criteria:
Bank account holders aged 18 to 50 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Age > 50.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account.
