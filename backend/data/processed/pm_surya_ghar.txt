SCHEME TITLE: PM Surya Ghar
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmsgmby

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Up to 300 units free electricity per month via rooftop solar installation with 60% subsidy.
Eligibility Criteria:
Households with suitable rooftop & electricity connection.
Exclusion Criteria:
Commercial buildings.
Documents Required:
Electricity Bill, Aadhaar, Bank Account.
