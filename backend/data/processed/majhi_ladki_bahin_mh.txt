SCHEME TITLE: Majhi Ladki Bahin MH
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Maharashtra)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Monthly financial aid of Rs. 1,500 to women aged 21-65 years.
Eligibility Criteria:
Women domiciled in Maharashtra with family income <= 2.5 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income tax payers.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Domicile Certificate, Ration Card.
