SCHEME TITLE: PM Kusum Solar Pump
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps for Farmers)
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
60% subsidy for installing standalone solar agriculture pumps & solarizing grid pumps.
Eligibility Criteria:
Farmers, Panchayats, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-farmers.
Documents Required:
Land documents, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
