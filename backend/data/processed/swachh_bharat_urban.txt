SCHEME TITLE: Swachh Bharat Urban
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Swachh Bharat Mission - Urban 2.0
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Financial assistance of Rs. 12,000 for constructing Individual Household Latrines.
Eligibility Criteria:
Urban households with no toilet facility.
Exclusion Criteria:
Commercial buildings.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account, Photo.
