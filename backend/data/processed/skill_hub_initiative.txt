SCHEME TITLE: Skill Hub Initiative
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Skill Hub Initiative (PMKVY in Schools)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Integrates vocational skill education in schools and higher education institutes.
Eligibility Criteria:
Students from Class 6 to 12 & out-of-school youth.

None.

School ID, Aadhaar.
