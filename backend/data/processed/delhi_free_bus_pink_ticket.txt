SCHEME TITLE: Delhi Free Bus Pink Ticket
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Delhi Pink Ticket Free Bus Scheme
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Free travel for women in all DTC and cluster city buses in Delhi.
Eligibility Criteria:
All female passengers traveling in Delhi city buses.
Exclusion Criteria:
Male passengers.
Documents Required:
None (Pink Ticket issued on bus).

• Domicile Proof
