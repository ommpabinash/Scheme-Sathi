ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal)
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Provides functional household tap connection to every rural home.
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural households across all states.
Exclusion Criteria:
Urban commercial.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Address Proof.
