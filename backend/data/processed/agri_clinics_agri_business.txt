SCHEME TITLE: Agri Clinics Agri Business
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Agri-Clinics and Agri-Business Centres (ACABC)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
45 days free training + 36-44% composite subsidy for agri-graduates.
Eligibility Criteria:
Graduates in agriculture and allied subjects.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-agri graduates.
Documents Required:
Agri Degree Certificate, Aadhaar.
