SCHEME TITLE: Free Silai Machine
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Free Sewing Machine Scheme (States)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Free sewing machines to poor women to start home-based tailoring business.
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural & urban poor women aged 20-40 years with family income <= 1.2 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income > 1.2 Lakh.
Documents Required:
Income Certificate, Aadhaar, Photo.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
