SCHEME TITLE: Sukanya Samriddhi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Tax-free savings scheme for girl child education & marriage.
Eligibility Criteria:
Girl child aged up to 10 years, opened by natural/legal guardian.
Exclusion Criteria:
Age > 10 years.
Documents Required:
Birth Certificate, Guardian ID.

SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof
