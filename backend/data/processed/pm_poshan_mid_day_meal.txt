SCHEME TITLE: PM Poshan Mid Day Meal
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme)
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Provides hot cooked meals to elementary school children (Class 1 to 8).
Eligibility Criteria:
Students enrolled in Government and Government-aided schools.
Exclusion Criteria:
Private un-aided schools.
Documents Required:
School Enrollment.

• Domicile Proof
