SCHEME TITLE: Chiranjeevi Health Rajasthan
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Mukhyamantri Chiranjeevi Swasthya Bima (Rajasthan)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Health insurance cover up to Rs. 25 Lakh per family per year for Rajasthan residents.
Eligibility Criteria:
All families domiciled in Rajasthan state.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-residents.
Documents Required:
Jan Aadhaar Card, Ration Card.
