SCHEME TITLE: PM Fasal Bima
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Comprehensive crop insurance against natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest.
Eligibility Criteria:
All farmers including sharecroppers & tenant farmers.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-notified crops.
Documents Required:
Land record, Bank passbook, Sowing certificate.
