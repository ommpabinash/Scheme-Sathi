SCHEME TITLE: Startup India Seed Fund
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/sisfs-fs

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Financial assistance up to Rs. 50 Lakh to early-stage startups for proof of concept.
Eligibility Criteria:
DPIIT recognized startups incorporated <= 2 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-DPIIT registered entities.
Documents Required:
DPIIT Recognition Certificate, Pitch Deck.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
