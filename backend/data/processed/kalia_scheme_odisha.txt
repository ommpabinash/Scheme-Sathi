SCHEME TITLE: Kalia Scheme Odisha
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
KALIA Scheme (Odisha)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Financial support of Rs. 25,000 per family for small/marginal farmers & landless agricultural laborers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Small, marginal farmers & landless agricultural laborers in Odisha.
Exclusion Criteria:
Taxpayers, MPs/MLAs.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Ration Card, Bank Account.
