SCHEME TITLE: PM Vishwakarma
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmv

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Vishwakarma Scheme
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Toolkit incentive of Rs. 15,000 and loans up to Rs. 3 Lakh at 5% interest for artisans.
Eligibility Criteria:
Artisans in 18 traditional trades (carpenters, potters, blacksmiths, etc.).
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-traditional trades.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Skill verification.
