SCHEME TITLE: PM Suraksha Bima
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Accidental death and disability insurance cover of Rs. 2 Lakh for Rs. 20/year.
Eligibility Criteria:
Bank account holders aged 18 to 70 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Age > 70.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account.
