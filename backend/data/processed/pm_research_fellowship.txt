SCHEME TITLE: PM Research Fellowship
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Research Fellowship (PMRF)
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Attracts top talent to Ph.D. programs at IITs/IISc with Rs. 70,000-80,000 monthly stipend.
Eligibility Criteria:
B.Tech/M.Sc graduates from IITs/NITs with high CGPA/GATE score.
Exclusion Criteria:
Low CGPA candidates.
Documents Required:
Degree certificate, GATE score, Research Proposal.
