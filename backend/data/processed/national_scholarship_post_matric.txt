SCHEME TITLE: National Scholarship Post Matric
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC Students
Category: Education & Scholarships
Objective:
Financial support to SC/ST/OBC students studying at post-matriculation stage to complete higher education.
Eligibility Criteria:
- Belong to SC/ST (Income <= 2.5 Lakh) or OBC (Income <= 1.5 Lakh).
- Enrolled in recognized post-secondary institutions.
Benefits:
- Full tuition fee waiver plus monthly maintenance allowance.
Documents Required:
- Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, Previous Marksheet, Aadhaar-linked Bank Account.
