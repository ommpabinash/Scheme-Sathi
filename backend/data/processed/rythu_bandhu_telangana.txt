SCHEME TITLE: Rythu Bandhu Telangana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Rythu Bandhu Scheme (Telangana)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Investment support of Rs. 10,000 per acre per year for agricultural inputs.
Eligibility Criteria:
Landowning farmers in Telangana state.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-landowning tenant farmers.
Documents Required:
Pattadar Passbook, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
