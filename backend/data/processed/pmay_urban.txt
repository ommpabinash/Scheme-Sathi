SCHEME TITLE: Pmay Urban
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmay-u

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U 2.0)
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Financial assistance for urban households to build or purchase pucca houses.
Eligibility Criteria:
EWS (Income <= 3L), LIG (3-6L), MIG (6-18L) owning no pucca house.
Exclusion Criteria:
Property owners.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Income Proof, Bank Details.
