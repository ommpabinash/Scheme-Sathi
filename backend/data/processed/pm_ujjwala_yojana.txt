SCHEME TITLE: PM Ujjwala Yojana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY 2.0)
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Deposit-free LPG gas connection + Rs. 300 subsidy per cylinder refill for poor women.
Eligibility Criteria:
Adult women from BPL / SC / ST / PMAY households.
Exclusion Criteria:
Existing LPG connection holders.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Ration Card, Bank Account.
