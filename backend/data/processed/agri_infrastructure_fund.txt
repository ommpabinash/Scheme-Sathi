SCHEME TITLE: Agri Infrastructure Fund
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Medium-long term debt financing for post-harvest management infrastructure.
Eligibility Criteria:
Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, Agri-entrepreneurs, SHGs.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-agri activities.
Documents Required:
Project report, Bank Account, Aadhaar.
