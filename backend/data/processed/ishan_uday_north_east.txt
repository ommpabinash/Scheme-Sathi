SCHEME TITLE: Ishan Uday North East
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Ishan Uday Scholarship for North Eastern Region
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Rs. 5,400 to 7,800/month for general/degree/professional students of NER.
Eligibility Criteria:
Students domiciled in NER states with family income <= 4.5 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-NER domicile.
Documents Required:
Domicile Certificate, Income Certificate.
