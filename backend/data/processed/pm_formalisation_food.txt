SCHEME TITLE: PM Formalisation Food
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing (PMFME)
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
35% subsidy (max Rs. 10 Lakh) for micro food processing enterprises.
Eligibility Criteria:
Individual micro food processors, FPOs, SHGs.
Exclusion Criteria:
Large food processing industries.
Documents Required:
Udyam, Aadhaar, Project Report.
