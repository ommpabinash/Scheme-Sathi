SCHEME TITLE: Working Women Hostel
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/wwhh

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Sakhi Niwas (Working Women Hostel)
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Safe and affordable hostel accommodation for working women.
Eligibility Criteria:
Single, widowed, divorced working women with income <= 50,000/month.
Exclusion Criteria:
High-income corporate executives.
Documents Required:
Employment Certificate, Income Proof.
