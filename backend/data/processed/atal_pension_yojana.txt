SCHEME TITLE: Atal Pension Yojana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/apy

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Guaranteed monthly pension of Rs. 1,000 to 5,000 for unorganized workers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Citizens aged 18-40 having a bank account. Must not pay income tax.
Exclusion Criteria:
Taxpayers.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account.
