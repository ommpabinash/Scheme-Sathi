SCHEME TITLE: Green Credit Programme
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Green Credit Programme (GCP)
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Market-based mechanism incentivizing voluntary environmental actions (tree planting, water conservation).
Eligibility Criteria:
Individuals, SHGs, Panchayats, Private Companies.
Exclusion Criteria:
None.
Documents Required:
Green Credit Portal Registration.
