SCHEME TITLE: PM Poshan Abhiyaan
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-poshan

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission)
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Reduces stunting, under-nutrition, anemia among young children & women.
Eligibility Criteria:
Children (0-6 years), pregnant women, lactating mothers.
Exclusion Criteria:
None.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Anganwadi Registration.
