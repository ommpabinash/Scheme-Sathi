SCHEME TITLE: Subhadra Yojana Odisha
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Subhadra Yojana (Odisha)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Financial assistance of Rs. 10,000 per year (total Rs. 50,000 over 5 years) to women.
Eligibility Criteria:
Women in Odisha aged 21 to 60 years from low-income families.
Exclusion Criteria:
Taxpayers, Govt employees.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, Domicile.
