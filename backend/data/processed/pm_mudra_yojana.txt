SCHEME TITLE: PM Mudra Yojana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmfmpe

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Collateral-free micro loans up to Rs. 10 Lakh (Shishu, Kishor, Tarun).
Eligibility Criteria:
Non-farm micro entrepreneurs, small shopkeepers, traders.
Exclusion Criteria:
Defaulters.
Documents Required:
Identity Proof, Business Proposal.
