SCHEME TITLE: PM Laghu Vyapari Pension
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
National Pension Scheme for Traders (NPS-Traders)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Pension of Rs. 3,000/month for small shopkeepers & retail traders.
Eligibility Criteria:
Retail traders & shopkeepers (GST turnover <= 1.5 Crore) aged 18-40.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income tax payers.
Documents Required:
GSTIN, Aadhaar, Bank Account.
