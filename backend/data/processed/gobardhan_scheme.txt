SCHEME TITLE: Gobardhan Scheme
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/gobardhan

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
GOBARdhan Scheme (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources)
Category: Clean Energy & Utilities | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Converts cattle dung and organic waste into Biogas and Compressed Bio-Gas (CBG).
Eligibility Criteria:
Farmers, Gram Panchayats, Dairy Cooperatives.
Exclusion Criteria:
None.
Documents Required:
Land details, Panchayat resolution.
