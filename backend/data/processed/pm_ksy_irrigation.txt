SCHEME TITLE: PM Ksy Irrigation
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Expands cultivable area under assured irrigation (Har Khet Ko Pani).
Eligibility Criteria:
Farmers with agricultural land.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-agricultural land.
Documents Required:
Land documents, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
