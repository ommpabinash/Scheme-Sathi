SCHEME TITLE: Stand UP India
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/sui

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Stand Up India Scheme
Category: Entrepreneurship & MSME | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Bank loans between Rs. 10 Lakh to Rs. 1 Crore for SC/ST and Women entrepreneurs.
Eligibility Criteria:
SC/ST and/or Woman entrepreneurs setting up greenfield enterprises.
Exclusion Criteria:
Existing businesses.
Documents Required:
Caste Proof, PAN, Business Plan.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
