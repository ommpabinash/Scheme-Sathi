SCHEME TITLE: PM Janman Tribal
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan (PM-JANMAN)
Category: Minority & Tribal Welfare
Objective:
Comprehensive development of Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs) residing in remote habitations.
Eligibility Criteria:
- Households belonging to recognized PVTG tribal communities.
Benefits:
- Pucca housing, clean drinking water, road connectivity, mobile medical units, and electrification.
Documents Required:
- ST Certificate / PVTG Community ID, Aadhaar Card.
