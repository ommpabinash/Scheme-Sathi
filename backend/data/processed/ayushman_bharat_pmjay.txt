SCHEME TITLE: Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ab-pmjay

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Free health cover up to Rs. 5 Lakh/family/year for secondary & tertiary hospitalization.
Eligibility Criteria:
Families listed in SECC 2011 & all senior citizens aged 70+.
Exclusion Criteria:
High-income categories.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar Card, Ration Card.
