SCHEME TITLE: Naps Apprenticeship
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Provides stipend support (25% up to Rs. 1,500/month) for trade apprentices.
Eligibility Criteria:
Youth aged 14+ having ITI / Diploma / 10th / 12th qualification.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-registered candidates.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Educational Qualification.
