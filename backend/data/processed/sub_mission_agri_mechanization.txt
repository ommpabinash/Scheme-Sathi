SCHEME TITLE: Sub Mission Agri Mechanization
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Provides subsidies for purchasing farm machinery and establishing custom hiring centers.
Eligibility Criteria:
Small, marginal, SC/ST, and women farmers.
Exclusion Criteria:
Commercial non-farming entities.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Land Document, Machinery Quotation.
