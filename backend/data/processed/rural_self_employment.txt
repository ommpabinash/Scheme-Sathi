SCHEME TITLE: Rural Self Employment
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mgsvry

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
RSETI (Rural Self Employment Training Institutes)
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Short-term residential skill training & bank loan facilitation for rural youth.
Eligibility Criteria:
Rural youth aged 18-45 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Urban non-poor.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, BPL Ration Card.
