SCHEME TITLE: Mgnrega
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/mgnrega

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
MGNREGA Rural Employment Guarantee
Category: Employment & Skill Development | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Guarantees 100 days of manual wage employment per year for rural households.
Eligibility Criteria:
Adult members of rural households.
Exclusion Criteria:
Urban residents.
Documents Required:
MGNREGA Job Card, Aadhaar, Bank Account.
