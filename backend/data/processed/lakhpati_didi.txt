SCHEME TITLE: Lakhpati Didi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ldyuk

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Lakhpati Didi Scheme
Category: Women & Child Welfare | Type: Central & State
Objective & Overview:
Empowers rural Self-Help Group (SHG) women to earn >= Rs. 1 Lakh/year.
Eligibility Criteria:
Women members of DAY-NRLM Self Help Groups.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-SHG members.
Documents Required:
SHG ID, Aadhaar, Bank Details.
