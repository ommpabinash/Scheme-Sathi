SCHEME TITLE: Paramparagat Krishi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pkvy

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Promotes organic farming through cluster approach and Participatory Guarantee System.
Eligibility Criteria:
Farmers forming clusters of 50 or more.
Exclusion Criteria:
Chemical farming clusters.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Land Certificate, Bank Account.
