SCHEME TITLE: Pragati Scholarship Girls
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girl Students
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Rs. 50,000/year for girls pursuing technical degree/diploma education.
Eligibility Criteria:
Girl students admitted to AICTE approved technical courses (Income <= 8 Lakh).
Exclusion Criteria:
Income > 8 Lakh.
Documents Required:
Admission Proof, Income Certificate, Aadhaar.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:

• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof
