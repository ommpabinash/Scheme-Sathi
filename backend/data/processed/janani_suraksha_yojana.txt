SCHEME TITLE: Janani Suraksha Yojana
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/jsy1

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)
Category: Healthcare & Wellness | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Promotes institutional delivery among pregnant women with cash assistance.
Eligibility Criteria:
Pregnant women from BPL/SC/ST families.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-BPL in high performing states.
Documents Required:
BPL Card, Aadhaar, Bank Account.
