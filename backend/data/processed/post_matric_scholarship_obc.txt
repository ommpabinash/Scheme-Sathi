SCHEME TITLE: Post Matric Scholarship Obc
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Financial support for OBC students pursuing post-matriculation studies.
Eligibility Criteria:
OBC students with family income <= Rs. 1.5 Lakh/year.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income > 1.5 Lakh.
Documents Required:
OBC Certificate, Income Proof, Marksheet.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
