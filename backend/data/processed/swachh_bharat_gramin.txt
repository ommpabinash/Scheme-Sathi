SCHEME TITLE: Swachh Bharat Gramin
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Swachh Bharat Mission - Gramin
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Incentive of Rs. 12,000 for BPL/identified APL rural households to build toilets.
Eligibility Criteria:
BPL rural households, SC/ST, small farmers.
Exclusion Criteria:
High-income households.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Bank Account.
