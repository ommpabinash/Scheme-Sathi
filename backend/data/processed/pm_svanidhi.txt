SCHEME TITLE: PM Svanidhi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-svanidhi

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
Category: Housing & Infrastructure | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Collateral-free working capital loans of Rs. 10k, 20k, 50k for street vendors.
Eligibility Criteria:
Street vendors with Certificate of Vending.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-vendors.
Documents Required:
Vending ID, Aadhaar Card.
