SCHEME TITLE: Nsap Disability Pension
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension (IGNDPS)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Monthly pension support for severely disabled BPL individuals.
Eligibility Criteria:
BPL persons aged 18-79 with severe disability (80%+).
Exclusion Criteria:
Disability < 80%.
Documents Required:
Disability Certificate, BPL Card, Aadhaar.
