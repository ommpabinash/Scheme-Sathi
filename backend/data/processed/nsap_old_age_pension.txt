SCHEME TITLE: Nsap Old Age Pension
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/upoaps

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension (IGNOAPS)
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Monthly pension for BPL elderly citizens (60+ years).
Eligibility Criteria:
BPL citizens aged 60 and above.
Exclusion Criteria:
Non-BPL households.
Documents Required:
BPL Card, Age Proof, Bank Account.
