SCHEME TITLE: Lakshmir Bhandar WB
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Lakshmir Bhandar Scheme (West Bengal)
Category: State Flagship Schemes | Type: State Government
Objective & Overview:
Monthly financial assistance of Rs. 1,000 (General) and Rs. 1,200 (SC/ST) to women.
Eligibility Criteria:
Female head of household aged 25-60 years domiciled in West Bengal.
Exclusion Criteria:
Government employees.
Documents Required:
Swasthyasathi Card, Aadhaar, Caste Certificate.
