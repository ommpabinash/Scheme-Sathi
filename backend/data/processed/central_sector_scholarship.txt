SCHEME TITLE: Central Sector Scholarship
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship (CSSS)
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Scholarship for top 20th percentile college students from low-income families.
Eligibility Criteria:
Class 12 pass students with family income <= 4.5 Lakh.
Exclusion Criteria:
Students receiving other central scholarships.
Documents Required:
Class 12 Marksheet, Income Certificate, Aadhaar.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
