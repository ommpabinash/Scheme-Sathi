SCHEME TITLE: E Shram Card
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/fasuw-ecw

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
e-SHRAM Portal Registration
Category: Social Security & Pensions | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
National database of unorganized workers providing accidental insurance cover of Rs. 2 Lakh.
Eligibility Criteria:
Unorganized workers aged 16-59 years.
Exclusion Criteria:
Income tax payers, EPFO/ESIC.
Documents Required:
Aadhaar, Mobile linked with Aadhaar, Bank Account.
Page 1


SCHEME BENEFITS:


DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
• Aadhaar Card
• Bank Passbook
• Domicile Proof

APPLICATION PROCESS:
Visit official website www.myscheme.gov.in to apply.
