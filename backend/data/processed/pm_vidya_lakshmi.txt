SCHEME TITLE: PM Vidya Lakshmi
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
PM Vidya Lakshmi Education Loan Scheme
Category: Education & Scholarships | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Single window education loan portal with interest subsidies for higher education.
Eligibility Criteria:
Indian students securing admission to recognized courses.
Exclusion Criteria:
Unrecognized private diplomas.
Documents Required:
Admission letter, Fee structure, Marksheet.
