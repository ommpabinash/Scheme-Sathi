SCHEME TITLE: National Beekeeping Mission
PORTAL URL: https://www.myscheme.gov.in

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
Government of India / State Welfare Scheme - Official Information
National Beekeeping & Honey Mission (NBHM)
Category: Agriculture & Farmers | Type: Central Sector
Objective & Overview:
Promotes holistic development of beekeeping for sweet revolution.
Eligibility Criteria:
Individual beekeepers, SHGs, Cooperatives.
Exclusion Criteria:
Unregistered entities.

Aadhaar, Beekeeper registration proof.
